Hydroelectric Dam – Build Template

Hariyana Vidya Mandir · Smart City model. Cut lines, fold lines and sizes for the dam wall, its four sluice gates, the spillways, the control station and the two water turbine wheels. Every size scales with your base board.

Scale 1.00× of the 110 cm digital model. Measure your river where the dam crosses it and type that into "Dam wall length"; the gates and spillways spread to match.

To print at true size: open this page in Chrome or Edge on a computer, press Ctrl+P, choose A4 landscape, set Scale to 100% (or "Actual size"), and turn off "Fit to page". Check the 5 cm test bar with a ruler before cutting.
